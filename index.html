02
A LETTER FOR YOU

My Baby, Thero...

Happy birthday to my Baby, my Big Daddy, my Spiderman, and one of the most beautiful parts of my life.

Today is about celebrating you — the person you are, the person you are becoming, your dreams, your heart, your smile, your strength, your softness, and even all the little things you do that you probably don't realise I notice. I hope you wake up today knowing just how loved you are. I hope you feel special, appreciated, wanted and celebrated, because you deserve every bit of it.

I hope you have the most amazing birthday, Baby. I hope today gives you reasons to smile until your cheeks hurt. I hope you laugh, eat your favourite food, enjoy every little surprise and allow yourself to be spoiled. But most importantly, I hope you remember that your birthday is not just a celebration of another year. It is a celebration of the fact that the world got you — and somehow, I got lucky enough to have you in my life.

I want you to know something: your birthdays will never be ordinary again.

Not while I am here. Every birthday we get to share is going to be magical, thoughtful and full of little pieces of our story. I want every year to have its own memory. I want photographs we can look back at, jokes only we understand, adventures we never planned, food we absolutely over-order, and moments that make us stop and think, "Wow, look how far we've come."

I want to celebrate every version of you. The happy you. The tired you. The ambitious you. The silly you. The stubborn you. The quiet you. The Big Daddy you. The Spiderman you. All of you.

I want to be there for the chapters nobody else sees and cheer the loudest when you accomplish the things you've been working toward.

Thank you for every memory we already have. Thank you for every conversation, every laugh, every hug, every random moment, every look, every silly thing that somehow became a memory I treasure. Thank you for being someone I can love, annoy, laugh with and make beautiful memories with.

I hope this next chapter of your life is kind to you. I hope it brings opportunities that make you proud, people who genuinely love you, peace when you need it, courage when things get hard, and happiness that stays long after your birthday candles disappear.

And when another birthday comes around, I hope we are still here — older, wiser, probably still being ridiculous, but still choosing each other.

I hope we look at this website and laugh at how everything started. I hope we have hundreds more photographs to add, hundreds more memories to make, and a lifetime of birthdays to celebrate.

So happy birthday, my Baby.
My Big Daddy.
My Spiderman.
My favourite person.

I love you more than this little movie could ever explain.

Forever yours ♥
